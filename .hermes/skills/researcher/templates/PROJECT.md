# PROJECT: <product or feature name>

This is ideation input, not a specification. Fragmentary, rough, or
contradictory content is expected — the researcher skill turns it into
documentation. Delete sections that do not apply; leave gaps rather than
guessing.

One-liner:

## What are we exploring?

The problem, the itch, the opportunity. Free-form notes welcome.

## Who might care?

People or roles, and what they are trying to get done.

## Ideas

Raw ideas, hunches, and "what if" thoughts. Bullets and half-formed notions are
fine.

## Capabilities we might build

Things the product could do — big or small, near-term or far-off. Include
capabilities you are unsure about.

## Scenarios / jobs to be done

Situations where this would matter, told as concretely as you can.

## Inspiration / references

Comparable products, articles, screenshots, prior art, or things you want to
learn from.

## Open questions

Unknowns, risks, and threads worth pulling.
